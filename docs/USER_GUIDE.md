# User Guide

## Verify a battery

You do not need to connect a wallet to verify a battery.

1. Open Battery Passport.
2. Enter the battery serial number.
3. Select **Verify battery**.
4. Review current status, health score, ownership summary and lifecycle history.

## Connect a wallet

Connect Freighter only when you need to perform an authorized lifecycle action. The application never asks for a secret key or seed phrase.

After connection, the workspace shows actions relevant to your role and the selected battery.

## Manufacturer

Use **Create passport** to register origin metadata. The manufacturer wallet becomes the initial owner.

## Inspector

Select a battery and add a 0–100 health score plus a concise inspection note. A score below 60 moves the battery to **Under review**. A later passing inspection returns it to **Active**, but does not automatically verify it.

## Verifier

A battery can be verified only after at least one passing inspection. Recalled or recycled batteries cannot be verified.

## Owner

The current owner can transfer ownership or request recycling with an authorized recycler.

## Recycler

When an owner selects your authorized recycler wallet, connect that wallet and approve the request. Recycling is not completed until the owner performs the final confirmation.

## Recall authority

Use a recall only for a genuine lifecycle/safety reason. Recall removes a prior verified status and blocks future verification.
